## Choose an entry point

| Your interest | Suggested starting points |
| --- | --- |
| Make AI coding work easier to follow and review | [Taskrivet](https://github.com/hamanakam1/taskrivet), [Panther Intent Capture](https://github.com/hamanakam1/panther-intent-capture), then [fluxrecord](https://github.com/hamanakam1/fluxrecord). |
| Inspect a dashboard or interactive product experience | [MeterMouseGarden](https://hamanakam1.github.io/meter-mouse-garden/) and [Murmuration](https://hamanakam1.github.io/murmuration/) have browser entry points. |
| Understand a subtle failure before building an integration | Run [Counterfactory’s example](https://github.com/hamanakam1/counterfactory#three-minutes-to-a-counterexample); explore [El Verificador](https://github.com/hamanakam1/el-verificador) for implementation checks. |
| Test business workflows without real customer records | Start with [MockMill](https://github.com/hamanakam1/mockmill) and the [StoryFoundry fixture](https://github.com/hamanakam1/storyfoundry-public-fixture). |
| Review local and distributed systems work | Start with [The Surveyor](https://github.com/hamanakam1/the-surveyor) and [VortexMesh](https://github.com/hamanakam1/VortexMesh), then follow the experiments below. |

These are reading paths through related capabilities. The [role diagram](https://hamanakam1.github.io/diagrams/factory-roles.html) distinguishes project responsibilities from proposed cross-project handoffs.
